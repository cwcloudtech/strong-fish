Anyone can open an account. The only decision to make is **which kind**.

## Athlete or coach

At sign-up you are asked to pick one:

* **I'm an athlete.** You follow the programs a coach writes for you, log your
  sets, and post to your clubs' feeds. This is the default, and it is what most
  accounts are.
* **I'm a coach.** As well as everything an athlete can do, you create clubs,
  write and import programs, assign them to your members, and add movements to
  the shared exercise catalog.

## Signing up as an athlete

1. Open [strong-fish.com](https://strong-fish.com) and choose **Sign up**.
2. Fill in your name, surname, email and a password.
3. Leave **I'm an athlete** selected.
4. Confirm your address from the email you receive.

That is the whole flow. You are in.

Nobody has to approve you, and you do not need a club to start: record your
maxes under **My 1RMs** and the app is already useful. A club is what connects
you to a coach.

## Signing up as a coach

The first four steps are the same - pick **I'm a coach** at step 3.

![The sign-up form with "A coach" selected](../../static/img/screenshots/signup-coach.png)

What differs is what happens next:

1. Your account is created **as an athlete**, and works immediately as one.
2. A request is queued for a superadmin, and every superadmin is emailed.
3. When they confirm it, your account becomes a coach and you are emailed. The
   **Clubs** screen gains a *create a club* button, and **Exercises** becomes
   editable.
4. If they turn it down, you are emailed the reason. Your account keeps working
   as an athlete.

:::info Why the wait
Choosing "coach" records a claim, not a grant. A coach writes other people's
training and can see the feedback they log, so the app does not take the claim
at face value - somebody confirms it first.
:::

### While you are waiting

Nothing is blocked. Log your maxes, join a club you were invited to, post to the
feed. When the confirmation lands, everything you did as an athlete is still
there - it is the same account with more it can do.

## The badges on your profile

Your profile carries two of them, side by side under your name, each in its own
colour.

**What you are here** is the first: *athlete*, *coach* or *superadmin*. You do
not pick this one - it follows your account, and it changes by itself the day a
coach request is confirmed.

**What you lift** is the second, and that one is yours to choose:

| Badge | For |
| --- | --- |
| Squat specialist | the lift you are known for is the squat |
| Bench specialist | ... the bench press |
| Deadlift specialist | ... the deadlift |
| Balanced totaler | no single lift carries you - the total does |

Pick it under **Settings → My specialty** on the web, or on the profile tab in
the app. It is optional, you can change it whenever you like, and *no specialty*
is a perfectly good answer.

It is a claim, not a calculation. StrongFish never works it out from your maxes:
somebody who has entered none is still entitled to say what they are, and a bad
squat day should not relabel anybody.

## Joining a club

Two ways in, and they are different acts:

* **A coach adds you.** You are simply in the club, and you get an email saying
  so.
* **A coach invites you.** The invitation waits under **Invitations** until you
  accept or decline it. It also arrives by email.

An invitation is addressed to your **email address**, not to your account, which
means a coach can invite you before you have signed up: create the account with
that same address and the invitation is waiting on your first visit.
