# strong-fish

A powerlifting app: **RPE-based programs whose loads are computed from each
athlete's own current 1RM**, clubs to coach them in, and a training feed.

Nothing derived is ever stored. Every set's weight is worked out from the
member's current max at the moment they read it, which is what makes "update
your 1RM and the whole program recalculates" true rather than a feature that has
to be maintained.
